# Commercial Execution Recipes & Delivery Patterns

Operational delivery recipes for `vibe-cook`.

---

## 1. Zero-Friction Landing Page Recipe

When cooking Phase 3 (Sales Funnel & Checkout):
- **Framework:** Keep it to 1 single scrolling page.
- **Components:**
  1. Eyebrow badge + Benefit H1 headline + Subhead + Primary CTA button.
  2. Social proof ticker or testimonial quote cards.
  3. "Old Way vs New Way" comparison grid.
  4. What you get (Offer Stack & Deliverables).
  5. Transparent pricing card with money-back guarantee badge.
  6. Direct embedded Stripe Checkout button or dynamic VietQR scan code.
  7. 5 Accordion FAQs.

---

## 2. 5-Day Launch Email Sequence Recipe

- **Day 1 (The Problem):** Why the old way is failing you right now.
- **Day 2 (The Discovery):** How I found this new mechanism to get [Result] in 1/10 time.
- **Day 3 (The Case Study):** Exact walkthrough of how a client/user solved [Pain Point].
- **Day 4 (The Offer Reveal):** Introducing the offer + 3 high-value bonuses + risk reversal.
- **Day 5 (Deadline / Urgency):** Founding member pricing closes tonight at midnight.
