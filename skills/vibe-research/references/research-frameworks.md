# Business Research Frameworks & Competitor Teardown Models

Operational guidelines for conducting commercial intelligence with `vibe-research`.

---

## 1. The Value Gap Matrix

Evaluate competitors across 4 dimensions to find the commercial wedge:

| Dimension | Market Incumbent | Our Wedge Opportunity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Speed to Value** | 2-4 weeks onboarding, complex setup | <60 seconds, instant delivery |
| **Pricing Model** | \$1,000+/mo enterprise lock-in | \$49-\$199/mo transparent subscription |
| **Feature Complexity** | 50 bloated features, high cognitive load | 1 focused task executed flawlessly |
| **Customer Support** | Slow ticket response, offshore queues | Direct founder access, concierge onboarding |

---

## 2. Customer Objection Mining Queries

When searching for real user complaints about existing tools/agencies, use these query patterns:
- `"[Competitor Name] alternative" site:reddit.com`
- `"[Competitor Name] complaints" OR "canceled my subscription"`
- `"looking for a tool that does" [Industry / Task]`
- `"how do you handle" [Pain Point] "without spending"`

---

## 3. Pricing Benchmark Formula

$$\\text{Recommended Price} = \\min(\\text{Cost of Inaction} \\times 0.10, \\text{Incumbent Price} \\times 0.50)$$

- Always anchor your price against the **cost of doing nothing** or the **cost of hiring an employee/agency**.
