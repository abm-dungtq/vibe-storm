# Funnel Audit & Diagnostic Checklists

Diagnostic procedures for auditing digital sales funnels with `vibe-scout`.

---

## 1. Landing Page Friction Checklist

Check every sales page against these 7 conversion killers:
- [ ] **Above the Fold Clarity:** Does a visitor understand WHAT this is, WHO it is for, and WHY they need it in under 5 seconds?
- [ ] **Mobile Responsiveness:** Does the primary CTA button render above the fold on iPhone / Android?
- [ ] **Social Proof Placement:** Is real customer proof visible before the first pricing tier?
- [ ] **Pricing Transparency:** Are prices clearly stated with zero hidden surprises at checkout?
- [ ] **Page Load Speed:** Does the page load in under 2.5 seconds on a 4G connection?
- [ ] **Frictionless Form Fields:** Are you asking for only essential info (Name + Email + Card/QR)?
- [ ] **Risk Reversal:** Is there an explicit guarantee (e.g. 14-day 100% money back)?

---

## 2. Drop-off Severity Classification

- **Critical (Red):** Drop-off exceeds 50% above industry benchmark. Immediate work blocker.
- **Warning (Yellow):** Drop-off is 15-30% below benchmark. Secondary optimization priority.
- **Healthy (Green):** Conversion matches or exceeds industry top quartile. Do not touch.
