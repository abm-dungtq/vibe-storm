# Standalone Copywriting & Design Prompt Packs

Use these standalone prompt templates to execute copywriting and design tasks without requiring any external AgentKit skills.

---

## 1. Landing Page Copy Master Prompt

Paste this prompt into Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor to generate a high-converting landing page:

```text
You are a world-class direct-response copywriter specialized in developer tools and micro-SaaS.
Write full landing page copy for: [INSERT PRODUCT / VIBE PITCH].

Structure required:
1. Above the Fold:
   - Eyebrow badge: [Audience Category]
   - H1 Headline: Clear transformation in under 10 words.
   - Subheadline: How it works + the primary pain point eliminated.
   - Primary CTA: High-intent button text (e.g. "Start Free 7-Day Trial").
   - Risk Reversal: (e.g. "No credit card required. Cancel anytime.").
2. Social Proof Bar: Logos / placeholder testimonials.
3. The Pain Section (Old Way vs New Way):
   - 3 bullet points detailing the friction of the traditional approach.
   - 3 bullet points highlighting the 10x speed of the new solution.
4. Core Features & Capabilities: 3 feature cards with screenshot callout descriptions.
5. Interactive Demo / Sandbox preview block.
6. Transparent Pricing Table: Monthly & Annual plans with highlighted "Most Popular" tier.
7. FAQ: 5 real objections answered directly.
8. Final CTA Banner.
```

## 2. Cold Outreach & Smoke Test DM Script

```text
Hey [Name], saw your post about [Pain Point / Workflow].

Quick question: If there were a tool that [Solves Pain Point in 30 seconds], would that be worth \$19/month for your team?

I just finished an early prototype and am looking for 5 founders to test it and give brutal feedback. No pitch, just want to see if this actually saves you time.

Open to a 60-second Loom link?
```
